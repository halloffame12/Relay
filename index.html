[ PRODUCT INTRODUCTION ]

Everything you need to keep your documents close.

Relay combines a focused, offline reading library with direct peer-to-peer file sharing. No cloud account to create, no third-party servers storing your books, and no manual IP addresses or port numbers in the normal flow.

01

Offline-First Storage

Your books and papers reside in local app storage managed by Drift SQL. Reading never touches a network.

02

Zero-Setup QR Pairing

A single QR code carries ephemeral handshake tokens. Both devices establish a direct socket without typed hostnames.

03

Explicit Receiver Consent

No file bytes are accepted until the receiver explicitly reviews the document name, size, and SHA-256 hash.

04

Bounded Streaming Memory

Files stream in bounded 64 KiB buffers with on-the-fly checksum calculation. RAM usage remains minimal regardless of file size.

[ CORE CAPABILITIES ]

Engineered for clarity and independence.

Four pillars designed to make reading and sharing as direct and trustworthy as handing a physical book to a friend.

[ 01 / READING ]

Read anywhere. Offline by default.

Import documents once through the system picker. Read on a plane, subway, or in the woods. Relay saves your exact scroll location and bookmarks directly in on-device storage.

  • /PDF Reader: Continuous rendering, instant page jump, and position persistence.
  • /EPUB Engine: Chapter navigation with bounded inline PNG, JPEG, and GIF rendering.
  • /Safety Guard: SVG and remote network images are strictly skipped to prevent remote tracking.
Verified: Never contacts remote font servers or analytics during reading.
[ 02 / SHARING ]

Share with a scan. No manual pairing codes.

Show an ephemeral QR code on the receiving device and scan it with the sender. Relay validates the payload strictly as Relay binary data—never as a web URL.

  • /Single-Use Tokens: QR invitations expire in 10 minutes and rotate immediately after transfer.
  • /Camera Security: Camera permission is requested only while the QR viewfinder is active.
  • /Manual Fallback: Local IPv4 and port details remain accessible when camera hardware is unavailable.
Verified: 5 failed manual code attempts invalidate and rotate the session token.
[ 03 / CONNECTIVITY ]

Across networks. Designed for the real world.

Designed to work whether both devices share the same Wi-Fi router or operate across different networks. Relay prefers a direct LAN socket and can fall back to WebRTC data channels when direct paths are blocked.

  • /Direct First: Fast local socket transfer over LAN whenever available.
  • /WebRTC Fallback: Encrypted data channel fallback for complex NAT topologies.
  • /Transparent Status: Clear in-app indicators displaying whether the transfer is LAN-direct or relayed.
Honest status: Cross-network relay requires an active signaling service (`infra/signaling`). Without it, transfers run local LAN.
[ 04 / INTEGRITY ]

Built around your files. Local-first & verified.

Relay manages its own storage copy so removing a book from Relay never damages your original file. Every incoming document is validated byte-by-byte before it touches your library.

  • /SHA-256 Checksum: Receiving device verifies matching hash before committing to storage.
  • /Duplicate Detection: Identical files are flagged to prevent duplicate storage consumption.
  • /Zero Partial Mess: Any dropped or interrupted transfer purges temporary `.part` files immediately.
Verified: Interrupted transfers leave zero orphaned files and zero broken database rows.

[ WORKFLOW ]

Understand it in three steps.

No accounts to authenticate. No router ports to forward. Just three clear steps.

01

Select

Choose PDFs or EPUBs from your offline Relay library. Relay creates a manifest with file sizes and cryptographic checksums.

Payload cap: 20 files / 200 MiB batch
02

Scan

The receiver taps "Offer to receive" to display a short-lived QR code. The sender scans it to establish an authenticated direct socket.

Token expires: 10 min • Auto-invalidates
03

Transfer

The receiver reviews the document details, confirms approval, and accepts the streamed bytes. The file is verified and added to the library.

Integrity: SHA-256 match required
[ ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES ]

Built on simple, rigorous rules.

Software that respects your device, battery, and cognitive load.

01

Simple Connections

Pairing is a QR scan. No pairing PINs, discovery broadcasting delays, or manual IP entry in the standard path.

02

Efficient Transfers

Files stream with bounded memory buffers. Only small control frames and manifests are kept in RAM.

03

Reliable File Handling

An interrupted transfer leaves zero partial files in your library. Temporary `.part` files are immediately purged.

04

Offline Reading

Your library and reading progress are stored in Drift SQLite. The app opens and functions without an internet connection.

[ PRIVACY BY DESIGN ]

Your documents should stay yours.

"We believe software should never treat your reading list or personal documents as marketing telemetry."

Relay keeps your documents on your device. Direct transfers travel device-to-device over your local Wi-Fi. No documents are ever uploaded to cloud storage or scanned for advertising.

We do not claim end-to-end encryption for plain local LAN sockets (which run raw TCP). When cross-network WebRTC data channels are utilized, packets are DTLS-encrypted in transit.

No Cloud Storage

Files never transit through or rest in AWS S3, Google Cloud, or third-party buckets.

Explicit Approval

Receivers must explicitly approve each file. No silent transfers or auto-downloads.

Ephemeral Handshake

QR invitations expire in 10 minutes and cannot be reused or replayed.

Zero Analytics

No tracking SDKs, no behavioral cookies, no user telemetry.
