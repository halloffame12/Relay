1. What Relay is

Relay is an Android application for reading PDF and EPUB documents stored on the device, and for transferring documents between Relay devices using a QR-initiated connection. It is provided as a release candidate and may change.

2. Your documents and your responsibilities

You are responsible for the documents you import, send and receive. Only transfer material you have the right to share. Relay does not scan documents for malware and does not verify that a sender owns the rights to a file.

Keep your own backups. Relay is not a backup service. Removing a document, clearing app data or uninstalling the app can remove Relay's managed copy.

3. Network paths and security

Local network transfers use Relay's own TCP path and are not encrypted at the transport level. WebRTC paths are DTLS-encrypted. Use trusted networks for sensitive files, and verify the sender and file list before approving a transfer.

Cross-network transfer requires a signaling service that you or an operator configure. Relay does not guarantee that a direct connection is available on every network, and does not promise that a relay will always be reachable.
